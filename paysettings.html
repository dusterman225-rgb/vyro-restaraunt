Get paid in crypto

Customers scan a QR code in the Vyro app: on the kiosk, on the screen at the counter, or on the small code printed on their check. Money goes straight to your wallet and you receive the full amount. The customer pays Vyro's small 1% service fee on top, inside the Vyro app.

Payments to a wrong address can't be reversed. Double-check it.
Honest limit: the payment is found by the page that is open, and until Vyro's server-side checking is added, a staff member with technical skill could mark a Vyro payment as received without it being real. Compare your wallet's incoming payments with the day's close report.
